Project: LOKORA 2.0 – AI-Powered Local Tourism Discovery & Smart Travel Planner
Brief Description of the Solution:

LOKORA is an AI-powered tourism platform that helps travelers discover hidden local destinations, explore cultural experiences, and create personalized travel itineraries based on their interests, budget, location, and available time. It connects tourists with authentic local experiences through smart recommendations, interactive maps, and an intelligent trip planner.

Problem We Aim to Solve:

Tourists often struggle to find reliable information about lesser-known destinations, discover authentic local experiences, and plan trips within their budget and time. Popular tourism platforms mainly highlight well-known attractions, leaving hidden gems and local communities underrepresented.

Our Solution:

LOKORA bridges this gap by making local tourism smarter, more personalized, accessible, and community-driven—helping travelers explore beyond popular tourist spots while supporting local culture and businesses.
